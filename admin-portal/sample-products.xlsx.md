# Sample Excel Format for Product Upload

## Required Columns:
- Name (required): Product name
- Category (required): Product category
- Price (required): Product price in ₹

## Optional Columns:
- Brand: Brand name
- OfferPrice: Discounted price in ₹
- Stock: Available stock quantity
- Description: Product description
- Image: Image URL

## Example Excel Structure:

| Name | Category | Brand | Price | OfferPrice | Stock | Description | Image |
|------|----------|-------|-------|------------|-------|-------------|-------|
| Basmati Rice 1kg | Grains | India Gate | 120 | 110 | 500 | Premium quality basmati rice | https://example.com/rice.jpg |
| Toor Dal 1kg | Pulses | Tata Sampann | 150 | 140 | 300 | Quality toor dal | https://example.com/dal.jpg |
| Atta 5kg | Flour | Aashirvaad | 250 | 230 | 200 | Whole wheat flour | https://example.com/atta.jpg |

Note: Create an Excel file (.xlsx) with these columns and data to upload products in bulk.
